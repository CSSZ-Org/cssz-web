It's match time! ⚽ **CSSZ – Czech and Slovak Students in Zurich** vs. **Czech & Slovak Union** ([Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/czechslovakunion/)) in a friendly football match at the Allmend in Zurich.

## Date & Time

**Thursday, October 22, 2026 at 17:00**

## Location

**Allmend II & III** – the football pitches right next to the **Züri Fit calisthenics / street workout park**, just behind the Saalsporthalle and across from Sihlcity.

## What to Expect

- ⚽ **Friendly match** – CSSZ vs. Czech & Slovak Union
- 👟 **All levels welcome**, and **women are very welcome** to play!
- 🍺 **Beer, drinks and snacks included**
- 👀 **Spectators welcome!** Not playing? Come watch, cheer and enjoy a beer with us
- 🎉 Good company and a chance to meet the other Czech & Slovak community in Zurich

## Getting There

From **Zürich HB**:

- 🚆 **S4** (from the underground SZU platforms) to **Zürich Saalsporthalle-Sihlcity** – only about 5 minutes
- 🚋 or **Tram 13** to **Saalsporthalle**

From the stop, walk past the Saalsporthalle onto the Allmend – the pitches are next to the outdoor workout park, just a few minutes on foot.

## What to Bring

- ✅ Sports clothes
- ✅ Football boots or sports shoes
- ✅ Shin guards (if you have them)
- ✅ Water bottle
- ✅ Warm layer for after the match (October evenings get chilly!)
- ✅ Team spirit! 💪

## Registration

Please [register here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe4fkls22YuAI-N17_7LjNYZOOb-6txq0gvTh12p2gpYm8sWw/viewform?usp=publish-editor) if you'd like to play, so we know how many people to expect and how much beer and snacks to bring! 🍻

See you on the pitch! 😊
