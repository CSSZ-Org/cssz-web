It's that time of year again! ♨️ This Saturday we're heading to the outdoor thermal baths in Baden for an evening of pure relaxation in 45°C water.

## Date & Time

**Saturday, October 17, 2026 at 19:00**

## What to Expect

- 🆓 **Free entry** - the baths are open to the public at no cost
- 🍷 **Wine, beer and snacks** will be provided
- ♨️ Hot thermal water (~45°C) under the evening sky

## Getting There

Take an SBB train from **Zürich HB** to **Baden** station. From there it's just a short walk to the [Heisse Brunnen](https://www.google.com/maps/place/,+Heisse+Brunnen/@47.4863649,8.2950497,16.04z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0x47906d18f358e1d7:0x36008e6a9cea1f3e!8m2!3d47.4815162!4d8.3135997!16s%2Fg%2F11s19r92qg). 🚆

## What to Bring

- ✅ Swimsuit
- ✅ Towel
- ✅ Warm clothes for afterwards (October evenings get chilly!)
- ✅ Flip-flops or sandals
- ✅ Good mood and a will to relax! 🎉

## Registration

Entry is free, but please [register here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd8aPI0h8u9JI1ie6broF_E3UW2syvNj9oGkBwzXzKr1jNmxw/viewform?usp=publish-editor) so we know how much wine, beer and snacks to bring! 🍻

If you have the time and the mood, see you there in the evening. 😊
