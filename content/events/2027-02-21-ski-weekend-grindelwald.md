Join us for an unforgettable 3-day ski weekend in stunning Grindelwald! 🏔️ We'll be staying at Chalet Teufi, a cozy mountain accommodation perfect for our group adventure.

## Dates

**Friday, February 21 - Monday, February 24, 2027**

3 nights, with plenty of time to explore Grindelwald's amazing ski slopes!

## Accommodation

We'll be staying at [Chalet Teufi](https://www.gruppenhaus.ch/en/house/holiday_house/chalet_teufi/748), a charming mountain chalet in Grindelwald.

**Capacity:** 23 people  
**Cost:** TBD

## Getting to the Slopes

The ski slopes are a short bus ride away from the accommodation - there's a bus stop nearby for easy access to the lifts. 🚌

## Costs (Estimated)

- **Accommodation:** ~CHF 50 per person (for 3 nights)
- **Train:** Zurich to Grindelwald return (~CHF 80 with Half-Fare Card)
- **Ski pass:** 3-day pass (~CHF 200)
- **Food:** Self-catered at the chalet + meals on the mountain

## What to Bring

- ✅ Your own bedding/sleeping bag and pillowcase
- ✅ Towels
- ✅ Ski/snowboard equipment (or rent on site)
- ✅ Food for shared meals at the chalet
- ✅ Warm clothes and good vibes! 🎉

## About Grindelwald

Grindelwald is one of Switzerland's most iconic ski destinations, located in the heart of the Bernese Alps with breathtaking views of the Eiger, Mönch, and Jungfrau. The ski area offers over 160 km of slopes suitable for all levels! 🏔️❄️
