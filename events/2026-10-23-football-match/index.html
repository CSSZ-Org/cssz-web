It’s match time! ⚽ CSSZ – Czech and Slovak Students in Zurich vs. Czech & Slovak Union (Instagram) in a friendly football match at ETH Hönggerberg.

Date & Time

Thursday, October 22, 2026 at 18:00

Location

ETH Hönggerberg – ASVZ, artificial turf / grass pitch (Kunstrasen / Rasenspielfeld).

⚠️ We’re still waiting for the booking to be confirmed. We’ll update this page with the exact pitch as soon as we know.

What to Expect

  • ⚽ Friendly match – CSSZ vs. Czech & Slovak Union
  • 👟 All levels welcome, and women are very welcome to play!
  • 🍺 Beer, drinks and snacks included
  • 👀 Spectators welcome! Not playing? Come watch, cheer and enjoy a beer with us
  • 🎉 Good company and a chance to meet the other Czech & Slovak community in Zurich

Getting There

Take the bus to the ETH Hönggerberg stop. The ASVZ sports facilities are right on campus. 🚌

What to Bring

  • ✅ Sports clothes
  • ✅ Football boots (suitable for artificial turf) or sports shoes
  • ✅ Shin guards (if you have them)
  • ✅ Water bottle
  • ✅ Warm layer for after the match (October evenings get chilly!)
  • ✅ Team spirit! 💪

Registration

Please register here if you’d like to play, so we know how many people to expect and how much beer and snacks to bring! 🍻
