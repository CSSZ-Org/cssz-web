Hot dogs (Párek v rohlíku)

Come and enjoy the spring vibes with a real Czech párek v rohlíku! 🌭🍻

 Event date:
8 May 2026, 17:30 — 21:30 (.ics file)
 Location:
KOL-K-Küche (outside if the weather is nice)
 Coordinator:
Max Kellenberger (max.kellenberger@cssz.ch)
 Registration:
Google Form

Do you also reminisce of the old days when life seemed oh so simple? 😌 When afternoons were filled with exploration and little adventures. One of those childhood adventures might just have been grabbing a hot dog otherwise known as “párek v rohlíku” and sitting in the sun talking about what cartoon you are going to watch when you get home.

If you want to experience a small taste of the past alongside that of a real párek v rohlíku (and Czech beer 🍻, of course), then you are wholeheartedly invited to come to this transformative experience, bring your friends and loved ones, it will be a feast unlike anything UZH has seen before.

We will be making the hot dogs in the UZH KOL kitchen with a possibility of moving the event to the outside if the weather were to be nice.

Do not forget to register in the Google Form, as there will only be a limited number of hot dogs available, first come 🏃‍♀️, first serve 🌭!
