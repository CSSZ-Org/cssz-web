CSSZ invites you and your friends to join the first CSSZ trip of the Autumn Semester 2022.

We’ll meet at the tram stop Triemli at 9:30 a.m. and start our trip in the direction to Uetliberg, continuing to Thalwil. There we’ll take the Ship 3731 29 to get back to Zürich Bürkliplatz (See). Expected duration of this hike with T1 level is 5:30 h.

Total distance: 19 km
Expected duration: 5:30 h
Uphill/downhill: 570 m
